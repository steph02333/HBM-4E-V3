## Completed data, clearly separate evidence tiers
- **100/100 DOE configurations**, **200/200 Q3D solves** completed, source status `SOLVED_UNVERIFIED_PHYSICS`; no recorded solver adaptive nonconvergence warnings. 3h42min observed walltime (17:13:29→20:55:30), 66.6sec/solve including overhead.
- **73/100 DOE points**: all applicable R3-vs-R5 C coefficients (`CAA`, `CBB`, and signed-matrix `|CAB|`; single-signal GSG only `CAA`) differ **<1%**. This is the numerical screen the professor actually requested: **'Demonstrate that further adaptive mesh refinement changes Cself and Cmutual by, say, <1%.'** It is a two-level result, not a proof of continuum limit; R3→R5 must result in meaningful adapted meshes.
- **27/100** have at least one coefficient differing >=1%; keep original labels and IDs, flag for targeted follow-up/uncertainty, *do not* silently replace difficult sample points.
- **10/100** fulfill both C difference <1% and an analyst-added >=10% change in **total mesh elements**. **CRITICAL: This >=10% mesh separation threshold is NOT a requirement from the professor, NOT an Ansys official convergence criterion, and must NOT be used to reject the other 90 as useless.** Indeed 63/73 C-under-1 points fall short of this extra screen, while 19/27 C-over-1 points have >=10% mesh separation. Zero duplicate total-mesh-size pairs; but total count alone cannot verify per-object mesh distribution.
- Median per-point maximum C difference 0.401%, P90 ~3.21%, highest 5.1177%. Median difference in actual total mesh elements 5.70%. Topology counts and risks in accompanying CSV.
- Worst observed numerical discrepancies: DOE0050 5.1177% mutual; DOE0047 4.9936% mutual; DOE0121 4.8292% mutual; DOE0062 4.8162% self; DOE0003 4.1252% mutual. Preserve as risk examples, NOT a definitive error estimate relative to exact physics.

## Professor-specific numerical validation
Professor email (2026-09) proposes: (1) analytical benchmark for simplified Cu/oxide coax; (2) adaptive mesh stability for Cself and Cmutual by ~<1% plus C symmetry/passivity, expected trends, charge conservation and sufficiently large domain; (3) independent solver at 10–20 representatives with approximate 2–5% cross-solver consistency. **Professor does not prescribe a 10%-increase-in-mesh-elements cutoff.** The threshold was an additional screening heuristic, not a necessary criterion. This project still has unclosed independent cross-solver same-BVP / closure / domain / model-physics gates; do not claim total physical validation merely from 73 paired screens.
