## Interpretation and caveats

- Q3D is 0.83013 fF greater than the ideal infinite/uniform-coax formula. Finite-length end fringing is a plausible contributor but **not proven** from the ZIP alone.
- Only a 1×1 net matrix is present. It cannot establish off-diagonal symmetry, TSV-to-TSV mutual coupling, or matrix passivity for a future multi-TSV model.
- Solver reported mesh convergence, but independent mesh-independence or boundary sensitivity is **not demonstrated** by a single completed setup.
- Geometry was checked from reported object and net names. We have **not visually inspected** the saved AEDT model geometry; optional screenshot or project review may identify end-cap details.
- This test is Cu / SiO2 / Cu coax, **not** a doped-Si/MOS TSV, and it does not validate bias-dependent capacitance or the final 3×3 array.
- The original summary CSV deliberately leaves C_Q3D and error blank pending independent inspection; this report computes them from the RAW matrix.
